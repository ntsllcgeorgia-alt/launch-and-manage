Day 0
Demo + greenlight.

Brand sprint.
Palette + type lock. Hero typography generated (~2 min). Three angles for the hero copy — you pick one.

12-second looping hero video + 4-6 section images + (Build/Custom only) preloader video. All custom, none stock.
Day 3
Build.
Site coded against the V2 stack: magnetic CTAs, reverse-loop video, scroll-reveal, mobile rebuild, performance-budgeted. Preview link sent to you.

Iteration.
You send feedback in plain English ("bolder accent in the hero"). Same-day changes. Sign off.
Day 5
Live.
Push to your domain. SSL active. We send you a Loom walking through the site. You go back to running your business.

No. 05  — Questions
Plate V

Questions.
Answers.

What's the catch with the free demo?
None. We build it because it's faster than convincing you on a call. If you don't love it, close the tab — we don't follow up unless you ask. If you do love it, we'll talk about keeping it as a real site (or one of our tiers). No card. No catch.
How long until I see my demo?
Under an hour during business hours (CT). If you submit after 9pm, it lands in your inbox by morning. You'll get one email when it's ready — with the URL, a screenshot, and a short Loom walking through it.
Can I cancel?
Yes — month-to-month, cancel anytime, no exit fees. We earn the renewal. If we're not earning it, you shouldn't pay for it.
Do you work outside Weatherford?
Yes. DFW, anywhere in Texas, and increasingly nationally for creative-class solopreneurs. Our roots are local; our delivery isn't. We just like to start with people we can drive to.
What if I don't like the first draft?
That's Day 4. You tell us what's off in plain English ("warmer accents, less serif, headline is off"). We change it same-day. We don't ship until you sign off.
Who actually builds my site (and my demo)?
Hazem (the founder, writing this) with a custom AI-assisted production pipeline. You get one human on the other end of the form, email, and DM. Not an account manager, not a junior, not an offshore team.
Do you use templates?
No. Every site is built from scratch in the V2 stack. The reason it ships in 5 days isn't templates — it's the pipeline we built to skip the parts other agencies bill you for.
Will my site rank on Google?
Yes — clean HTML, semantic markup, fast load, Lighthouse 75+, schema for local businesses, GBP integration on the contact page. We're not an SEO agency, but the basics are baked in.
